Your information

Privacy, explained plainly.

This policy describes what Goodstuff Designs collects, why we use it, who helps us process it, and the choices available to you.

Effective 30 August 2026

Responsible party

Goodstuff Designs, based in Heidelberg, Gauteng, South Africa, is responsible for personal information processed through this website and direct enquiries.

Privacy enquiries can be sent to admin@goodstuffdesigns.co.za or discussed on Call nowWhatsApp us.

Information we use

  • Information you choose to send by email or provide during a telephone conversation.
  • Basic technical and usage information such as page visits, browser type, approximate region, device characteristics, clicks and scrolling.
  • Your analytics-cookie choice, stored in an essential first-party cookie.

Do not send passwords, payment-card information, identity numbers, medical information or other unnecessary sensitive information through a general enquiry.

Why we use it

We use information to answer enquiries, provide requested support, scope services, operate and secure the website, understand site performance, improve content and meet legal obligations. We process information only where there is an appropriate justification, such as your consent, a requested service, a contract, a legal obligation or a legitimate operational need that does not override your rights.

Analytics

Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity help us understand website use. Analytics cookies are enabled only when you accept them. If you reject analytics, these services may still receive limited cookieless measurements under their consent modes, but they may not use analytics storage on this site.

Clarity uses Balanced masking, and contact or enquiry areas are explicitly masked. We do not intentionally send enquiry text, names, email addresses, telephone numbers or message subjects in analytics events.

Service providers

We use service providers where needed to operate the website and measure performance, including Vercel for website delivery, Google for Tag Manager and Analytics, and Microsoft for Clarity. These providers process information under their own service terms and privacy commitments.

Information may be processed outside South Africa. Where cross-border processing occurs, we take reasonable steps to use providers and safeguards appropriate to the information and service.

Retention and security

We keep information only for as long as reasonably needed for the stated purpose, legal requirements, disputes or security. GA4 event-level data is configured for 14 months. The consent-choice cookie lasts six months. Provider-specific retention can differ and is described in the Cookie Policy.

We use reasonable organisational and technical safeguards, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Withdrawing analytics consent is immediate for future storage. Use or contact us for another privacy request. Withdrawing consent does not invalidate processing already carried out lawfully.

Updates

We may update this policy when the website, service providers or legal requirements change. A material cookie-policy change will use a new consent version and ask visitors to choose again.
