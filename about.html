Who we are

Technology that behaves.

Goodstuff Designs is a technology studio in Heidelberg, Gauteng, South Africa. We keep business systems working, measurable and honest — from the network to the website to the AI workflow.

Heidelberg, Gauteng, South Africa · serving clients remotely worldwide

What we do

Goodstuff Designs (Pty) Ltd delivers managed IT support, business networks and fibre connectivity, cybersecurity built on Kaspersky endpoint protection with backups and incident response, business websites and e-commerce stores with managed Linux hosting, AI workflows and business integrations, and digital marketing across social media, Google Ads and Google Analytics.

We also build Goodstuff-Studio, a desktop AI workspace for Windows with agents, projects, skills and scheduled tasks.

How we work

Every engagement starts with a plain-language conversation about what needs to work. We quote fixed prices before work starts, document what we build, and hand over systems you own and understand. If AI is the wrong tool for a task, we say so.

Services are delivered on-site across the Vaal Triangle and East Rand, and remotely across South Africa and internationally.

Where we are

The studio is based in Heidelberg, Gauteng, South Africa. Business hours are Monday to Friday 08:00–17:00 and Saturday 08:00–13:00 (SAST, GMT+2). Critical managed-support issues target a response within four hours during business hours; general enquiries are answered within two business days.
