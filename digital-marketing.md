# Goodstuff Designs - Digital Marketing

Measurement and tracking, social media marketing, Google Ads management, and reporting with real return on investment. Digital marketing that starts with the numbers, not the noise. Based in Heidelberg, Gauteng, South Africa - serving businesses across South Africa and internationally.

## Measurement and tracking

GA4, Search Console, Tag Manager and conversion tracking, set up so the numbers can be trusted. Every marketing decision downstream depends on this layer, and it is the one most businesses skip.

- Google Analytics 4 configured, tested and consent-compliant
- Google Search Console verified, sitemaps submitted, coverage watched
- Google Tag Manager containers with clean, documented tags
- Conversion tracking: forms, calls, WhatsApp, purchases - whatever a lead is for you

Priced fixed per property. Starts with an audit of what is already installed, plus a short document listing every event and what it means.

## Social media marketing

Content planning, post creation, audience targeting and performance analysis on the platforms where customers actually are: Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube and X.

- Content planning: a calendar tied to your seasons, launches and offers
- Post creation: copy, imagery and short-form video built for each platform
- Audience targeting on boosted and paid posts - the people who buy, not everybody
- Performance analysis: reach that turned into enquiries, not just impressions

Priced monthly per platform. One platform done properly before the next, with a monthly report against the agreed goal.

## Google Ads management

Campaign strategy, keyword targeting, ad optimisation and budget management. Google Ads works when the targeting is honest: the right keywords, the right locations, landing pages that answer the search.

- Campaign strategy: search, performance max and local campaigns where they fit
- Keyword targeting with a real negatives list, so budgets stop leaking on wrong searches
- Ad optimisation: copy, extensions and landing pages tested against conversions
- Budget management with caps, pacing and a monthly spend report in plain language

A keyword and competitor review comes first: what customers actually search, what it costs per click, and whether the numbers can work. Management fee quoted separately from ad spend.

## Reporting and ROI

Lead tracking, conversion analysis, cost per lead and campaign return on investment.

- Lead tracking from first click to closed deal, across channels
- Conversion analysis: which campaigns and platforms actually sell
- Cost per lead and cost per sale by channel, in rands
- Campaign ROI reporting - keep, fix or kill decisions, stated plainly

The monthly report is one page: spend by channel, leads by channel, cost per lead, and what changed.

## How marketing starts

1. **Audit and measure** - what is running, a working measurement layer, and agreement on what a lead is worth.
2. **One channel properly** - the strongest numbers first, with a budget, a target and a review date.
3. **Report and expand** - monthly cost-per-lead and return reporting; channels that earn their place get more.
