The rules we build by.

These are not aspirations - they are how every AI system we hand over is structured.

01

Human control

Approvals, sensitive account actions, security decisions, billing commitments and final customer communication stay with your team until a safer operating model is proven. AI prepares; people decide.

02

Explainability

Every workflow has a written map: what triggers it, what data it reads, what it produces, and where a person checks the output. If it cannot be explained on one page, it does not ship.

03

Permissioning

Agents get the least access that does the job - scoped accounts, scoped data, scoped actions. Credentials live in your name, and access is revoked the day a workflow retires.

04

Honest boundaries

We will say plainly when a task should not be automated, when the accuracy is not good enough yet, and when a human does it better. A workflow that quietly fails is worse than no workflow.

05

Ownership

Documents, prompts, accounts and configurations are handed over in writing. You are never hostage to us knowing things you cannot see.

How an AI engagement runs.

The same four steps whether it is one workflow or a company-wide rollout.

Step 1

Map

We map the workflow in plain language: what happens today, where the hours go, and where mistakes happen.

Step 2

Set boundaries

We agree what the system may do, what it must never do, and where a person checks the output. In writing.

Step 3

Launch with oversight

The workflow runs on real work with a human checkpoint. We measure accuracy against the task, not the demo.

Step 4

Hand over or expand

Once it earns trust, we document it, hand it over, and move to the next task - or stop, if it should.

Before you ask.

The questions that come up on nearly every AI conversation.

Will AI replace my staff?

Not in the work we automate. The point is to remove the repetition around their jobs - the retyping, the summarising, the chasing - so the people you trust do more of the work you actually hired them for.

Where does my data go?

Where you decide. Workflows can run on hosted models, on your own infrastructure, or fully local with Ollama through Goodstuff-Studio. We agree the data boundary before anything is built, and it is written into the handover.

What does it cost?

Workflows are quoted per task after the map step, because scope is everything. There is no licence lock-in to us: the system, documents and accounts are yours from day one.

Do you work with businesses outside South Africa?

Yes. We are based in Heidelberg, Gauteng, and deliver remotely across South Africa and internationally. AI work suits remote delivery well - the map, the boundaries and the handover are all written artefacts.
