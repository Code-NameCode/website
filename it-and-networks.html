01 - Network administration

Routers, switches, WiFi, VLANs and firewalls - configured properly.

A business network is plumbing: invisible when it works, expensive when it doesn't. We design and administer the whole stack - router and switch configuration, business WiFi that reaches every room that needs it, VLANs that keep guest traffic away from your POS, and firewalls with rules someone can actually explain.

  • Router, switch and access point deployment and configuration
  • Business WiFi: coverage surveys, mesh design, roaming that works
  • VLAN segmentation for staff, guests, CCTV and card machines
  • Firewall rules, port forwarding done safely, VPN access for remote staff
  • Network troubleshooting: from a slow office to a down branch

Find the gaps before someone else does.

We run vulnerability assessments and security audits on your network and endpoints, supply and manage endpoint protection, and stand behind you when something goes wrong.

  • Vulnerability assessments across network, servers and endpoints
  • Security audits with findings ranked by actual risk, not fear
  • Endpoint protection supplied and managed, including Kaspersky via Voxos
  • Incident response: containment, recovery and a plain-language post-mortem

Before you ask.

The questions that come up on nearly every IT and network conversation.

Do you sell hardware too?

We specify it, source it through local distributors, and stand behind the installation. You are never locked into kit you cannot buy elsewhere.

Can you take over a network someone else installed?

Usually, yes. It starts with the coverage and configuration review - what is there, what is documented, what is risky - and a written plan to stabilise it.

What does "open source monitoring" actually mean for me?

No per-device licence fees and no vendor lock-in on the tools that watch your network. You own the stack; we run it and interpret it.

Do you support businesses outside Gauteng?

Yes. Remote support covers anywhere in South Africa, and remote project work covers international clients. On-site work focuses on the Vaal and East Rand.
