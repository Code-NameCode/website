# About Goodstuff Designs

Technology that behaves. Goodstuff Designs is a technology studio in Heidelberg, Gauteng, South Africa. We keep business systems working, measurable and honest — from the network to the website to the AI workflow.

## What we do

Goodstuff Designs (Pty) Ltd delivers:

- Managed IT support and business networks, including routers, switches, WiFi, VLANs, firewalls and monitoring.
- Business connectivity and fibre through Vox ISP — home FTTH from R349/month, business FTTB, fixed LTE-A/5G, fixed wireless, rural satellite and uncapped VoIP, handled from coverage check to installed line.
- Cybersecurity: vulnerability assessments, security audits, Kaspersky endpoint protection from R278/device/year, backups and incident response.
- Business websites from R5,000 and e-commerce from R8,500, plus managed Linux hosting from R100/month (Novice), R200/month (Business) and R600/month (Pro) with DirectAdmin, SSL and a 99% uptime target.
- AI workflows, business integrations, AI-assisted support and AI-powered marketing that stay explainable and audited.
- Digital marketing: GA4, Search Console and Tag Manager, social media, Google Ads management, and reporting on cost per lead and ROI.

We also build [Goodstuff-Studio](https://goodstuffdesigns.co.za/studio), a desktop AI workspace for Windows with agents, projects, skills and scheduled tasks.

## How we work

Every engagement starts with a plain-language conversation about what needs to work. We quote fixed prices before work starts, document what we build, and hand over systems you own and understand. If AI is the wrong tool for a task, we say so.

Services are delivered on-site across the Vaal Triangle and East Rand, and remotely across South Africa and internationally.
