How marketing starts here.

The same three steps whether it is one channel or the whole mix.

Step 1

Audit and measure

We audit what is running, install or repair the measurement layer, and agree what a lead is worth to you.

Step 2

One channel properly

We launch the channel with the strongest numbers first - social or Ads - with a budget, a target and a review date.

Step 3

Report and expand

Monthly reporting on cost per lead and return. Channels that earn their place get more; channels that do not get said so.

Before you ask.

The questions that come up on nearly every marketing conversation.

Do I have to run every channel?

No. One channel done properly beats four done badly. We usually start with measurement plus the single channel closest to your customers, prove the numbers, then expand if it makes sense.

Who owns the ad accounts and analytics?

You do. Every account - Google Ads, Analytics, Search Console, social profiles - is created in your name with you as the owner, and the credentials are handed over in writing.

How much should I budget for Google Ads?

It depends on what your clicks cost in your industry and area, which is exactly what the keyword review answers before you commit. We quote a management fee separately from your ad spend - the spend goes to Google, not to us.

Can you work with our existing website?

Yes. Tracking and campaigns work on any site we can add a tag to. If the site itself is the weak link - slow, unclear, hard to contact you on - we will say so, and we build those too.
