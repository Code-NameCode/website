# Goodstuff Designs - IT & Networks

Network administration, IT support, cybersecurity, network monitoring, AI for IT operations, IT infrastructure and managed business IT services from one team. Based in Heidelberg, Gauteng, South Africa - serving businesses across South Africa and internationally through remote delivery.

## Network administration

Routers, switches, WiFi, VLANs and firewalls, configured properly. A business network is plumbing: invisible when it works, expensive when it doesn't.

- Router, switch and access point deployment and configuration
- Business WiFi: coverage surveys, mesh design, roaming that works
- VLAN segmentation for staff, guests, CCTV and card machines
- Firewall rules, port forwarding done safely, VPN access for remote staff
- Network troubleshooting: from a slow office to a down branch

Scoped per site. Starts with a coverage and configuration review or a clean design for a new office, and ends with a network map and a written proposal.

## IT support

Windows, hardware, software and drivers, fixed by a person. Critical issues target a response within four hours during business hours.

- Windows 10 and 11 setup, migration, repair and hardening
- Hardware diagnostics, upgrades and replacement coordination
- Software installation, licensing and update management
- Driver conflicts found and fixed properly, not reinstalled blindly
- Remote support across South Africa; on-site across the Vaal and East Rand

## Cybersecurity

Find the gaps before someone else does.

- Vulnerability assessments across network, servers and endpoints
- Security audits with findings ranked by actual risk, not fear
- Endpoint protection supplied and managed, including Kaspersky via Voxos
- Incident response: containment, recovery and a plain-language post-mortem

An audit produces a written report: what was tested, what was found, what to fix first, and what it costs.

## Network monitoring

- Open source monitoring stacks deployed on infrastructure you own
- Uptime checks for sites, servers, mail and key network devices

- Automated alerts by email, WhatsApp or ticket, routed to a human

## IT infrastructure

Servers, backups, cloud and disaster recovery.

- Server deployment: on-premises, cloud, or a mix that fits the budget
- Backup systems with scheduled restore tests, not silent failures
- Cloud infrastructure on major platforms, configured and costed sensibly
- Disaster recovery planning: RPO and RTO agreed in writing, then rehearsed

## FAQ

- **Do you sell hardware too?** We specify it, source it through local distributors, and stand behind the installation. You are never locked into kit you cannot buy elsewhere.
- **Can you take over a network someone else installed?** Usually, yes. It starts with the coverage and configuration review - what is there, what is documented, what is risky - and a written plan to stabilise it.
- **What does "open source monitoring" actually mean for me?** No per-device licence fees and no vendor lock-in on the tools that watch your network. You own the stack; we run it and interpret it.
- **Do you support businesses outside Gauteng?** Yes. Remote support covers anywhere in South Africa, and remote project work covers international clients. On-site work focuses on the Vaal and East Rand.
