# Cookie Policy

Consent version 1, effective 30 August 2026.

The essential first-party cookie `gsd_consent_v1` stores `accepted` or `rejected` for six months.

If analytics is accepted, Google Analytics 4 may use `_ga`, `_ga_*`, `_gid`, or `_gat*`, and Microsoft Clarity may use `_clck`, `_clsk`, and related Microsoft-domain cookies. Exact provider cookies and duration can change with vendor configuration.

If analytics is rejected, `analytics_storage`, `ad_storage`, `ad_user_data`, and `ad_personalization` remain denied. Under Advanced Consent Mode, Google and Microsoft may still receive limited cookieless measurements.

Visitors can reopen Cookie settings from every page footer. Withdrawing consent updates vendor state, asks Clarity to clear its session, and removes accessible first-party analytics cookies.

Full policy: https://goodstuffdesigns.co.za/cookie-policy
