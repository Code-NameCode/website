Maison Vaucluse · 1911

Pieces made once,
and then not again.

Every stone in this house is cut, set and finished by hand in our Place Vendôme workshop, in numbers small enough that we know the name of the person who polished each one. We do not restock. We do not repeat a design. When a piece is sold, the drawing is retired.

The house

Four workshops,
one pair of hands each

We employ twenty-nine people. Nine of them are lapidaries. All of them have been here more than eight years, which is not a coincidence we plan for — it is the only way to keep a standard.

  1. The stone room

    Rough material is bought at auction in Anvers and Johannesburg, then held for between two and four years before a single design is drawn. Stones over 4 carats are bought individually and named.

  2. Cutting

    Eleven cutters, all trained in-house. A stone is rejected if it loses more than 8% in cutting, no matter how good the rough looked in the parcel.

  3. Setting

    Yellow, white and rose gold only, always 18 karat or higher, always from two refiners we have used since 1974. Platinum on request.

  4. The salon

    By appointment, on the second floor, with daylight. We will show you the drawing before we show you the piece. Most clients change their minds at that point, and that is fine.

Three materials, and the reason for each

Unheated sapphire

Heating improves colour and size. It also permanently changes the stone, and we would rather tell you the truth about what you are holding than improve it by 15%.

Two refiners

We have bought every gram of our gold from the same two houses since 1974. Full chain of custody is available for any piece, on request, in writing.

Set by hand

No machine presses, no laser welding. A setting takes between six and twenty hours depending on the stone, and it is the part of the work we will not delegate.
