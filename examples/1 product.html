06 — Questions

The things people actually ask.

Anything not covered here goes to a human: hello@meridian.audio, answered within a working day.

Headphone impedance alone does not decide it — sensitivity does. Start at 2 V for most 32–80 Ω headphones, drop to 0 V for very sensitive in-ears where the volume control has plenty of range, and use 4 V for 250 Ω planars. If you are still clipping the dial at 3 o'clock, the bias is too high; if you are past 1 o'clock, it is too low.

No. Volume, bias and input are all on the front panel and the unit plays music the moment it is plugged in. The app is for firmware, per-source trims, the measurement log and Roon setup — things you do twice a year, not things you do while listening.

The unit, a 2 m USB-C cable, walnut cheeks pre-fitted, a 15 W USB-C power supply, four bolts and a hex key, and a printed measurement sheet for the specific unit you received. There is no CD, no Quick Start guide, and no QR code leading to a five-step onboarding.

The front panel, dial, cheeks and USB-C port are all replaceable with two screws. Spares are sold at cost and stay available for ten years, which is the same promise we make about firmware. The five-year warranty covers the electronics, extendable to ten when you register the unit.

Sixty days, opened or not, with the unit in any condition. Headphones are harder to judge in a shop, so we would rather you took it home. If it is not right, we cover return postage and there is no restocking fee and no interrogation about why.
