What we hold ourselves to

The publication standard

Every piece in Contrast Journal is checked against this list before it publishes. If a piece breaks one of these rules, we publish it anyway and print the failure in a visible box at the top. That is the whole editorial policy.

There is no dark pattern here, no newsletter pop-up before the third scroll, no “reject all” next to “reject non-essential” with the same weight. When we use a reading preference toggle, it survives a page reload.

“An accessible site is not a special edition of your site. It is the site.” — from our founding editorial, issue 1, 2019
  1. 4.5:1 minimum, alwaysBody copy and its placeholders, with no exceptions granted for “brand”.
  2. Focus you can see at 1 metreA 3px solid ring with a 2px offset, never removed in CSS.
  3. 44px touch targetsMeasured including padding, not just the label.
  4. Real labels on every inputPlaceholder is not a label. Neither is an icon.
  5. Motion that stopsprefers-reduced-motion removes every animation, including ours.
  6. One page, no pop-upsNothing interrupts the reading column, ever.
  7. Tested by a person, not a linterAutomated checks are the floor. They are not the room.
