Position

Aarhus asked for a landmark. We proposed a long brick wall with seven arches and a public roof, which took four years and several public meetings to be accepted as less impressive than what was originally drawn.

Construction

Hand-pressed brick from a yard in Fåborg, lime mortar throughout, and a steel liner that carries the water load so the brick can stay thin. Twenty-six weeks on site for the shell, twenty-two for the fit-out.

Brief

Sixty-four social-housing apartments on a leftover plot, at a rent the municipality could defend to residents in a meeting. No podium, no car park, no shared corridors longer than six metres.

Outcome

Completed three months over the client's optimistic programme and four months under the tender figure. Ninety-one per cent of households stayed after three years against a local average of seventy-four.

Position

We are interested in the parts of a building nobody photographs

Stairs, thresholds, drainage, the way a wall meets the ground. These are the things that decide whether a building is still good in fifty years, and they are where our time goes.

We work slowly in the early stages and normally afterwards. We do not enter competitions we cannot staff with partners we trust, and we have turned down more work than we have taken.
