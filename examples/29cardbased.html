How a guide is built

Every guide here went through the same four gates.

  1. It came from a real project

    No guide exists here without a named project, a client and a thing that went wrong. The first draft is usually a debrief, not a method.

  2. A second practitioner checked it

    Every guide is read by someone who was not on the project. If they would not follow it on their own work, it is rewritten.

  3. The raw material is included

    Excerpts of real instruments, real code frames and the anonymised notes. A method you cannot see is a method you cannot copy.

  4. It is revised, not abandoned

    Each guide carries a revision date. When a project contradicts it, we change it and say what changed.
