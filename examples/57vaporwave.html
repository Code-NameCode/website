From the 1994 catalogue notes

A label for buildings that are open but empty

There is a sound that belongs to a place at a specific hour: the atrium of a shopping centre at 9:40pm, twenty minutes after closing, when the escalators have stopped but the air handling has not. Nobody is there. Everything is still lit. It is the most generous and the loneliest sound we know how to record.

Mallsoft Collective was started in 1994 to put that sound on a format you could hold. Thirty-two years later the buildings have mostly gone, and the sound has not, and neither has the label. We have pressed twenty-nine releases, sold about forty thousand units in total, and made a profit in eleven of those years, which we consider a fair result for a company that exists because a shopping centre stayed open too late.

We do not use synthesizers we cannot explain, we do not remaster anything, and we do not put anything on a record that we have not heard on a bad speaker in a car park. Artists keep their masters and their money. There are no contracts here, only handshakes, and we have only ever broken one, badly, in 2001.

Everything in the store is the complete catalogue. If a title is not there, it is because the pressing is finished, not because we are hiding it from you.

“Everything we have ever pressed is here, including the four that should not exist.”
Ilse Ranta, label head

Interview, Wire magazine, 2019. Ilse still has the original tape of the interview and will not release it.
