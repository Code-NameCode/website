Six workshops, fourteen pieces, no filler

Each piece lists the workshop that made it, the town it was made in, and what happens when it eventually wears out.

Material notes

The linen is grown in Normandy and finished in Guimarães

European Flax, rain-fed, no irrigation. It is spun and woven at a family mill that has been running the same looms since 1948, then washed twice so it arrives already soft.

It will soften further after ten washes. That is the point, not a fault.
