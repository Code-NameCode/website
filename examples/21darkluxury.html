The atelier

Eleven hands,
six weeks.

A Meridian leaves the bench only when one person can account for every surface. We do not run production lines. We run a room of eleven watchmakers, and we tell you which one signed your movement.

Your reference number is engraved beneath the balance cock, next to the date it was completed.

  1. Strip and inspect

    Every incoming movement is opened, photographed, and measured against our tolerance sheet. Roughly one in six is returned to the supplier.

  2. Finishing by hand

    Anglage, straight-graining, and perlage are applied with abrasive that has no equivalent on a production line. This is where the time goes.

  3. Six positions of regulation

    The watch is timed in six positions over forty-two days, twice a day. The final record travels with the piece.

  4. Delivery

    Presented in a fitted case with the regulation record, the maker's signature, and a service interval already booked for year eight.
