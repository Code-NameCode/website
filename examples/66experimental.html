Deposit a work

Open call, no fee, no gatekeeping committee. We review every submission within six weeks. If it fits the collection we mint a permanent URL and a reference number; if it doesn’t we tell you why, once, in plain words.

  • Accepted: digital works 1996 to now, plus anything you can prove you made this year.
  • Not accepted: NFT-only works, AI-generated work without a documented human process, or anything sent to us already published elsewhere.
  • We keep: the description you write, the date, and your name. Nothing else. No analytics, no third-party embeds without your say-so.

You can change this later. Please don’t put a file URL here.

Only used to tell you the decision.
