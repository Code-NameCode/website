Engagement model

What the first 30 days look like

We connect read-only across identity, cloud and endpoint. No alert volume, no tickets. You get a written map of what is actually exposed, who actually holds standing access, and where your logs stop. Week one is deliberately quiet.

Engineers write and tune detections in shadow mode against 30 days of your telemetry. Every rule is tuned until its precision beats 85%, then it is promoted. You approve the promotion list; nothing goes live without your sign-off.

Your on-call engineer and ours sit on a bridge for a full simulated incident. We hand over the runbook, the escalation tree and the phone tree. Monthly reporting starts with the same numbers you saw on day one, so improvement is legible.
