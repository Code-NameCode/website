How it is made

Eleven weeks, four people, one frame

Nothing here is automated except the sewing, and only because the sewing machine has been automated since 1974.

  1. Frame

    Kiln-dried beech, dowelled and glued, then a full day in the press. Rejected frames go to the firewood pile, not to a second chance.

  2. Suspension

    Eight-way hand-tied webbing. It takes an hour and it is the single reason the sofa outlives the cloth by a decade.

  3. Covers

    Cut from a single roll, sewn in one piece where possible, and shrink-tested on a 60 °C wash before it goes anywhere near a frame.

  4. Finish

    Legs turned and stained in the workshop, then the whole thing goes out on our own van and one of us stays behind to see it through the door.
