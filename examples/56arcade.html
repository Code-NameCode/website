House rules

What happens if I lose in round one?

You keep your entry fee as alley credits, which is roughly four plays on the mid-tier cabinets. This is deliberate: nobody enjoys watching a friend walk out after nine minutes.

Can I touch my opponent's controls?

No. This is the one rule that gets people thrown out, and the operators have been trained to enforce it. Everything else is a warning.

Is there a prize for second place?

A hoodie, and the knowledge that you were beaten by someone who has a hoodie. The hoodie is genuinely good, which is the problem.

Can I just come and watch?

Yes, and you should. Spectator entry is free, standing room is fine, and the commentary booth will explain what is going on for anyone who asks.
