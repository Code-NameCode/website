Questions small teams ask

Honest answers

Is Hearth a replacement for our accountant or payroll bureau?

No, and it is not trying to be. Hearth produces a clean, validated payroll file and hands it to whichever bureau you already use. Nine of our customers switched bureau after starting with us, which is a good sign.

We are six people and one of us does this in a spreadsheet already. Why change?

Because the spreadsheet quietly becomes a job. Hearth does not ask you to learn much: the most-used actions are approve, add a person, and check a balance. Most teams are fully migrated in an afternoon.

Where does our data live, and can we get it out?

In an EU region, encrypted at rest. Everything is exportable as CSV or JSON from settings at any time, including documents and the full audit trail. No exit fee and no email required.

What happens when we cross fifty people?

Nothing changes except the price. Studio exists for multi-entity and audit-heavy cases, not headcount. Most forty-person teams stay on Team.
