For the grown-ups

Captain Loop is made by people with children, which mostly means it is built around the same arguments.

  • No ads, no in-app purchases, no currency. One price, once. Progress lives on your device.
  • Weekly limits you set, not us. A session timer in grown-up settings, with a friendly end-of-round message rather than a lockout.
  • Names are not shown to anyone. There is no chat, no lobby, and no way for one child to message another.
  • Works with a keyboard. Every block is reachable by Tab, and the whole game can be played without a mouse.
