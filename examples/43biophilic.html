What we believe

Three beliefs

Daylight is medicine

We model every room through a year before drawing a line. Circadian light is not a styling note; it is the first material we lay down.

Air you can feel

Cross-ventilation, thermal mass, and honest ceilings. We specify airflow before we specify finishes, and we measure it after handover.

Materials that age well

Unsealed timber, lime plaster, stone that darkens. Nothing we specify needs replacing to stay beautiful, which is also the cheapest thing we can offer a client.

Case study 01

Ashgrove: a dining hall that ends at sunset

The clients asked for a retreat that felt quiet without feeling austere. The plan is one room with three thresholds, and the deepest one is a covered veranda you have to walk out onto to get your tea.

Structure is a single 34-metre larch frame, left unsealed so it silvers. Air moves through the hall by stack effect alone; on still July days the only mechanical system running is the kitchen extract.

  • BriefTwelve-room adults' retreat, year-round
  • Area680 m² across three thresholds
  • VentilationNatural stack, 100% of design days
  • Post-occupancyOccupants rate restfulness 9.1/10 at one year
