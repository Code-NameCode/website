Small studio, two wheels, one kiln

Everything here is thrown by two people who signed it.

We make about four hundred pieces a year in stoneware and speckled clay, fired in a wood kiln six times between March and October. Nothing is slip-cast, nothing is assembled from a mould, and nothing leaves that we would not eat from.

The process

How a pot gets made here.

Five stages, eleven days, and one firing that decides whether the piece is any good. The kiln has the final word and we have stopped arguing with it.

Open the kiln on firing night if you like. It gets loud.

1Wedging

Clay is cut from the block, wedged on a plaster bat, then wedged again the next day. This is unglamorous and it is the single thing that stops a pot from exploding in the kiln.

Twenty minutes per kilo, and we do not use a pug machine for anything on the shelf.

2Throwing

Thrown on two kick wheels, trimmed the following morning when the clay is leather-hard. Every piece gets a maker's stamp pressed into the foot before it ever sees heat.

Wall thickness is judged by hand, not by callipers. This is not a mistake.

3Handles and marks

Pulled handles are attached with score-and-slip and left to shrink at their own rate. Lettering is done in slip under a trailing liner, which is why a third of our marks wobble.

We stopped chasing even lettering in 2021 and have not looked back.

4Bisque and glaze

Bisque at 980°C over about fourteen hours, then a single glaze or slip, applied by hand in three coats. Waxed feet mean the kiln shelf marks never touch a table.

5The wood firing

Thirty-six hours of stoke-and-watch, two people alternating, ash carried up by the flame. Roughly one piece in five is lost to the fire and we do not count those as failures so much as tuition.

People ask why we don't just buy blanks. It is cheaper and it is faster and it produces a better mug, in the way a photograph of a mug produces a better mug.

A thrown pot records the person who made it — the slight lean, the pulled handle, the place where the wall thins. You cannot fake that by accident, and you cannot fake it on purpose either.
