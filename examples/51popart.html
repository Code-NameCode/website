Three Panels From The Lab

How a fizzy fountain drink became a working-hours staple.

Panel One: The Fountain

In 1984 our founder's grandmother ran a soda fountain in Sheboygan. Her citrus soda was the only thing on the menu that could get a dock shift done by 6pm. We rebuilt the recipe in a lab in 2018 and changed exactly one thing: the caffeine source.

It tastes like her fountain. Not like a lab.

Panel Two: The Bench

Every batch is tasted by three people before it ships. If it doesn't snap, it doesn't leave. We throw away about one in nine.

160mg caffeine per can, from a slow-dissolving guarana source so the lift arrives in a ramp, not a cliff.

Panel Three: The Print

The label is one colour plus black on unbleached board. No shrink wrap, no foil. The can is 82% recycled aluminium.

Printed two colour, four times a year, by a family shop in Sheboygan that has run the same press since 1958.
