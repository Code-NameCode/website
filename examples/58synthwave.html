What your pass gets you

Practicalities, because a festival site this size is mostly walkways.

  • Entry is via the west ramp. There is no step-free route to the rooftop; the ground floor is fully step-free and has the quiet tent, the water, and the food.
  • Lockers are free and inside the booking. Bring a padlock or a euro in coins, which nobody does and everybody regrets.
  • Cash only at the four food stalls. The rooftop bar takes cards and is the only place with a queue worth queueing for.
  • Water is free at four points on all three levels. Bring an empty bottle; there is nothing on the roof to fill one.
  • The 23:00 curfew is enforced by the city, not by us. The Level 2 disco continues with headphones, which is the correct compromise.
  • Photography is fine on the ground floor and not on the rooftop. Several artists have asked, and we have honoured all of them.

The Grid

Four decks, one core stair, and 1,100 parking spaces. The plan below is the same one taped to the entrance barrier, slightly weathered.

Getting there

Tram 4 to Parklaan, then four minutes on foot. No parking on site — it is a car park, but a full one. Cycle racks at the ramp, 300 spaces.
Levels

Five decks, one core stair, and two lifts that serve levels 1 to 3 only. The rooftop is a ramp, and it is a long one.
Quiet hours

23:00 curfew on the rooftop, all three nights. The ground floor stays quiet all weekend and never closes.
Weather

Bring a jacket. The rooftop is a rooftop. If it rains we move the Rooftop stage to level 2 and tell everyone twice.
