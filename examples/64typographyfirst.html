The typeface is the interface. Everything else is a decision about hierarchy, rhythm and how much space a sentence is worth.

16 pt

Halden was drawn for long-form reading on screens that are also desks, calendars and kitchen windows. Its large optical sizes are not an afterthought: at display sizes the joints open up and the serifs blunt, because a display cut that behaves like a text cut at 120 point looks like a spreadsheet wearing a costume.
