Fabric and making

Where it's made

Two factories: one in Guimarães for outerwear, one in Łódź for knitwear. We visit both, twice a year, and we publish the dates.

What's in it

Recycled nylon, deadstock cotton twill, merino blend, waxed canvas. 82% recycled or deadstock across the drop by weight.

How we price

Margin roughly 2.1× landed cost. We publish it because someone asked once, and the answer turned out to be useful to other people.

After the drop

No restock, no "second drop", no archive sale. When a size goes, it's gone, and we make the next thing instead.
