Service & ownership

Consoles are only good if someone keeps them honest.

Maintenance

Fitted by

Halden Audio Works, Bristol. The console was rebuilt in 2019 from two dead desks and has run every session since.

Service interval

Faders and pots rebuilt every five years. Last: March 2024. Next: March 2029.

Known quirk

Channel 6 fader has a light scratch at 70 % of travel. It is in the log, not a fault.

Warranty

Two years on parts, five on the frame. Consoles that are actually used should not need either.
