We draw the section before we draw the building.

Studio Kern works on the parts of a city that were built once and then neglected: depots, clinics, laundrettes, the concrete floors nobody wanted. We begin with a cut through the existing structure, because that is the only drawing that tells you whether the work is worth doing.

Project 01 · in progress

Salt Depot Repair

A 1930s salt store on the Maashaven, 4,100 m², unused since 1998 and structurally sound in a way that surprised everyone including us. We are inserting a floor of workshops and a public salt library, and leaving the ground floor exactly as found, including the stains.

Practice

Eighteen people, two buildings, no growth target

What we do

  • Repair and conversion before new build, always
  • Section-first feasibility studies, four weeks
  • Public realm, particularly where nobody goes
  • Conservation to listed standards where the fabric earns it

What we will not do

  • Demolition without a structural survey attached
  • Render, tiling or cladding over existing concrete
  • Residential work above eight storeys
  • Competitions we cannot afford to build the winner of

How we charge

  • Open book, always, including our overheads
  • 18% of construction cost, never a percentage of cost
  • Feasibility studies fixed at €14,500
  • We publish our margin in every set of accounts
