The platform

Four layers, one timeline, no guesswork

Most tools hand you a map. Aeris hands you the map, the uncertainty band, and the decisions that changed because of it — so an analyst and a board member read the same object differently without arguing about the picture.

Hydro — where the water actually is

Soil moisture, snow water equivalent, river stage and groundwater, on a single 2.1 km grid that updates every six hours. Reservoirs, not just riverbanks, because a full river tells you nothing about a dry basin.
