Case study

Nordvik Marine: 41 deliveries a day, one window

Nordvik supplies spare parts to 60 vessel berths along the Norwegian coast. Halvorsen replaced four carriers with a single overnight trunk and a scheduled shuttle from Kvadrat.

  • −31% cost per delivery, achieved by consolidating four carriers onto one trunk lane
  • 98.8% of berth deliveries inside the customer-promised two-hour window, up from 81%
  • Single API replacing three order-entry portals — Nordvik's own team now books in under 40 seconds
  • Contract to 2031 with indexed fuel and wage clauses agreed up front, so no mid-term surprises
