Questions people actually ask

Yes, and it is the most common setup. Each household gets its own view; items can be marked "shared" so they appear on both, with each parent seeing only the tasks assigned to them. Reminders respect each household's own quiet hours.

Child accounts are deliberately thin. They see their own schedule, their own chores, and a yes/no done button. Anything involving appointments, purchases, or private notes is hidden by default and has to be explicitly shared by a grown-up.

You export everything as CSV or ICS from the settings screen, at any time, without emailing anyone. If you simply stop paying, the account stays readable for a year and then closes. We do not sell household data, and we do not run ads.

It handles the eleven most common school formats, including the ones written in Comic Sans by a parent at 11pm. You always get a review screen before anything is added, and anything it is unsure about is flagged rather than guessed.
