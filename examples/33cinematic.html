Meridian GT

A grand tourer built around one decision: put the driver in the middle of the noise, not on top of it. 612 horsepower, six speeds, one very long bonnet.

Request a drive Full specification

Chapter oneWhere the weight sits

Design

Drawn from one line and interrupted twice

The Meridian's profile is a single unbroken line from the front arch to the tail, interrupted only by the door shut line and the rear light blade. The windscreen sits 62 mm lower than the outgoing car, which is why the driver looks out rather than through.

Drag coefficient is 0.24. That number is the argument for everything else on this page.

Powertrain

The loudest thing in the car is the door

There is a 4.0 litre twin-turbo V8 and a six-speed manual, and both are quieter than you expect. Acoustic glass, a laminated floor, and body seals tuned against a recording of a V8 at full song. The result is that when you do open the door, the car is suddenly, absurdly loud.

612 hp at 6 800 rpm. 590 lb ft from 2 200. Rear transaxle, 48% of the mass behind the driver.
