The features

Everything on this page is included on every plan.

No feature is held back for an upgrade. Plan differences are volume and support response, nothing else.

Rates that compare every carrier, on every parcel

Parcel asks all nine carriers for a live rate the moment an order is created, and shows the cheapest by total landed cost — including the surcharges that usually appear three weeks later.

Returns without the paper

Issue a return label from the order page, print a QR code or hand out a freepost sleeve. The refund fires the moment the parcel is scanned back in, not when finance remembers to check.

How returns work

Tracking that answers the question

Not a map with a dot. Each event is written in the words your customer actually asked for, and it is publishable to your own site.

Billing that reconciles itself

Carrier invoices are matched to parcels line by line. Discrepancies over £2 are raised with the carrier and tracked to a credit.

Address checking that stops redeliveries

Every address is validated, normalised and geocoded at checkout, with the confidence score kept on the record so your support team can see why a parcel went astray.

One record, every carrier

A parcel handed to a different carrier mid-journey keeps its history, its reference and its customer-facing tracking page. Nothing restarts, nothing is lost in a handover, and the timeline is the same object whichever network it is travelling on.

How it works

Four steps from order to doorstep.

  1. Connect the store

    Authorise Parcel against your storefront. Orders flow in as they are placed, with line items and customer details intact.

    parcel.orders.sync()

  2. Pick a rate

    Every carrier quotes in under a second. Rules choose one; a human can override, and the reason is kept.

    rule: weight < 2.5kg → cheapest

  3. Print or hand over

    Label to a home printer, a network label printer, or a QR code your customer shows at the point of collection.

    ZPL · PDF · QR

  4. Reconcile

    Carrier invoices arrive matched to parcels. Discrepancies are raised, chased and credited, automatically.

    £0 unmatched in July
