Approach

We design for the fiftieth year of a building, not the first photograph of it.

That means generous floor-to-floor heights, structures a local firm can repair without a specialist, and materials that weather into their own colour. It also means we say no to a lot of work — roughly two thirds of what is offered to us each year.

How the studio runs

Founded
2011 by Ingrid Halden and Petter Aas, in a former chandlery on Grønland
People
9 — six architects, one site manager, one model maker, one practice lead
Project load
Four concurrent projects maximum, typically 18–30 months each
Procurement
Competitions or direct appointment; we publish our fee proposal in full
Post-occupancy
Every completed building is revisited at 1, 3 and 10 years, uninvited and on our own time
