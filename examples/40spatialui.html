How depth works

Four planes, one ladder

Comet never invents a z-index. There are five rungs, each with its own shadow step and its own opacity, and a panel's rung is decided by what you are doing rather than by what it is.

Raising a panel does not scroll, does not reflow, and does not take focus away from what you were reading. It only changes four things at once: order, shadow, scale and dimming.

The ladder
TokenValueCarries
--z-bar60App bar, always above every plane
--z-rail50Depth rail
--z-panel-140The front plane
--z-panel-230Second plane
--z-panel-320Third plane
--z-panel-410Everything behind
