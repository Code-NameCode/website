DeadStatic

Four people, two pedalboards, one van that smells permanently of amp dust. We play short. We play loud. We have never released a song under three minutes and we are not planning to start.

Discography

Four releases. Nothing on a streaming service that wasn't already ruined by one.

Every record is a short run on cassette and vinyl, sold from the van or by post. When they're gone, they're gone — we do not repress, because then they'd just sit in a warehouse looking like product.

01

Floorboard

EP · 2021 · 11 tracks · 19 min

Recorded in a stairwell in a fortnight. The one people found, which has confused us for five years.

02

Half Light

LP · 2022 · 9 tracks · 31 min

Our only record with a song over four minutes. The label asked us to cut it. We said no, loudly, in a meeting we were then removed from.

03

Damp Course

EP · 2024 · 7 tracks · 14 min

Written in a rented flat with a leaking roof. We kept the rain, then removed it, then added it back in a gate.

New EP: Twelve Weeks of Nothing

Seven tracks, recorded live to tape in a barn outside Todmorden in one day. No overdubs, no click, one broken microphone that stays in the mix on track four because we liked how it made the room sound.

Merch

Printed on demand, in a bedroom, badly.

Screen-printed one at a time in a rota of four. Sizes run small; the shirts are boxy by accident and we stopped apologising for it. Everything's printed to order, so nothing sits in a cupboard for a year.
