The atelier

One maker, from first assembly to final regulation

Henri Marchand has assembled every Aurum since 2011. There is no assembly line; there is a bench, a set of tools he made himself, and a regulation run that takes four days.

  1. CasesTen hours of milling and hand-polishing per case, finished until it holds no fingerprint at arm's length.
  2. DialsPrinted in La Chaux-de-Fonds in two passes, then fired. A dial is rejected if the minute track varies by more than 0.2 mm.
  3. RegulationSix positions, fourteen days. The watch must hold its rate in every orientation before it is cased.
  4. SignatureThe maker's stamp and the series number are engraved inside the caseback, where you will need the picture to read it.
