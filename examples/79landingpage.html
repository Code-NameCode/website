What it is

Three problems, one device

We did not set out to build a portable power bank. We set out to stop one specific Sunday: the one where the power goes out, the router is dead and nobody knows whether the freezer door is still closed.

  1. 01

    The internet stops, the bills still arrive

    A dead router means an empty till and a card machine that times out. Tinderbox runs a router, a small switch and two handsets for three days, and recharges in daylight.

  2. 02

    A dead fridge is a food bill, not an inconvenience

    A 12 V output keeps a chest freezer, a fridge/freezer or a medical cooler within its safe range for 72 hours. It is the only thing on this list you may one day be very glad to own.

  3. 03

    Dark hallways, in a house full of people

    Four hours of LED lighting and a 12 V pump for a flooded basement, in a pack that weighs 1.1 kg and fits the side pocket of a rucksack.

How it works

Three moving parts

No app to install, no account, no firmware updates. It turns on when the grid stops and turns off when the grid comes back.

  1. Charge while you canLeave it in a window. Six hours of overcast daylight fills it; three hours of sun does it faster.
  2. Plug in what mattersTwo sockets, one USB-C and a 12 V output. It remembers the last three things you plugged in.
  3. It takes over automaticallyMains drops, and the outputs stay live within 400 milliseconds. Nothing to switch, nothing to notice.

Before you join

If something here is still unclear, email us and a person will answer it.

A power station is better if you have a car, a socket and 6 kg of lifting in you. It is worse if the thing you need to keep running is the thing that fails when the power goes: the router. Ours is built around that, which is why it is smaller, cheaper and recharges itself.
No. There is no deposit and no card. About one in ten people on the list changes their mind before launch, and we will simply contact the next person in the queue.
It has already slipped twice, because the cell supplier pushed certification back. If it slips again we will email everyone on the same day, and your place is kept either way. We would rather tell you late and honestly than on time and wrongly.
A 200 W chest freezer pulling 90 W average will run for roughly 48 hours on a full charge, and considerably longer if you leave the lid shut. We are conservative on purpose: the number on the box is 12 W continuous, not the 65 W peak from USB-C.
