How it works

Three steps, then it gets out of the way

1

Answer five questions

How much time you actually have, what your last week looked like, and what you would rather not do. Takes about ninety seconds.

2

Get one session a day

Chosen from four teachers and 240 sessions. Every third day repeats something you rated well, because novelty is not the point.

3

It notices, quietly

If you skip four days we shorten the next session by a minute. We never tell you we did, and there is nothing to dismiss.

Reasonable questions

Before you sign up for anything

Is there a free trial?

There is a free plan with two sessions a week and no card. The paid plan is £6 a month or £48 for the year, and you can cancel in two taps from inside the app. We do not do annual auto-renew traps — if you pay yearly, we remind you by email 14 days before.

Do you sell my data?

No. We do not run third-party analytics, we do not share session history with advertisers, and we have never sold a dataset because we do not have one to sell. Our only revenue is subscriptions. Our engineers can tell you exactly what leaves your phone, and the answer is your email to our server when you log in.

I have never meditated. Will this make me worse?

Some people find sitting still makes them more aware of their own thoughts, which is uncomfortable for about a week. If that happens, try one of Tomas’s counting sessions — the count gives your mind something to hold. If you would rather not, stop. Nothing is stored and nothing follows you.

Can I use it without headphones?

Yes. Every session has a voice-only version at the same quality, which is how a lot of people use it on a train. Two sessions per week are silent by design, with no music and no voice at all.
