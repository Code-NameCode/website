Field note · Westfjords, February

Nine weeks for two usable days

Location
Hornstrandir, north-west Iceland
Dates
2 January – 1 March 2026
Usable light
Two days, four hours total
Frames kept
Fourteen, from roughly forty thousand exposures
Equipment
Two bodies, three lenses, no tripod after week three

The weather is not an obstacle to the work. It is the work.

People assume the hard part is getting there. It is not. Getting there is a map problem and I enjoy map problems. The hard part is that the thing you came for — a specific quality of light on a specific coastline — happens for perhaps ninety minutes, twice, and you find out about the second one from a phone forecast that is wrong in a way no weather app is willing to be precise about.

So you drive to where it will be. Then you drive somewhere better. Then you wait, badly, in a car park, for six days.

What came out of it is fourteen frames. Eleven of them are grey. Three of them are the reason I keep doing this, and I still could not tell you at the time which three they were going to be — that is decided later, in a room with too much coffee, by looking at a contact sheet until one of them will not let go of you.

That is the whole method, and it does not scale, and I have stopped apologising for it.
