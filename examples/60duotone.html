The room

One converted grain store, one live room, one control room, and a colour separation bench that nobody is allowed to touch.

Everything is printed here, on a two-colour Heidelberg that has not been serviced since 2019 and refuses to die. We mix in mono, master in mono, and cut the lacquer knowing it will be pressed in two colours anyway, which means we mix for contrast rather than for detail. It is a different discipline, and it is why the records sound the way they do.
