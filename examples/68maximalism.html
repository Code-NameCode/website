The building

How it stacks up

We are four rooms, two stairwells and one very small lift, in a converted cold store from 1911. The complexity is not a metaphor, it is a fire risk assessment, and the layering is deliberate: you can be in four different kinds of noise without ever meeting the other three.

Access: step-free to Rooms A and B via the Goods Lift. Room C is up nine stairs and we will not pretend otherwise. Ear defenders free at the bar, every night, no questions.

The 340 Club

Two hundred and forty people, one membership, everything cheaper

  • £8 entry to any event instead of £10–£16
  • Your own mug, which lives behind the bar under your name, which is embarrassing in the best way
  • First refusal on Nowhere Good and every residency night
  • Bottomless coffee in the Grill, permanently
  • One guest pass a month, transferable, no questions
Join the 340 — £9 a month

There are 212 members and 212 mugs, because we lost two in a flood and have decided that is a founding myth rather than a loss. Membership is month to month, you can leave in a tap, and nobody from the venue is allowed to ask you why.

Tickets

Get in the door

We sell direct, at the door price, with a £1.50 booking fee and no resale. If it says sold out, it is sold out — we do not release returns.

One email with your ticket. If it is sold out we will tell you before you pay.
