Grimforge

A small, mean dungeon crawler

Thirty-two floors under a dead salt mine, drawn on a 16×16 grid and rendered without a single smooth pixel. Every room is hand-placed. Every death is your fault. There is no procedural anything, and that is the point.

Module 04

Two people, one compiler, no budget

Grimforge is made in a custom engine that draws rectangles. That constraint is the whole art direction, and it is not going away.

$ ./build.sh --floor 32 --tiles 16 ok rooms placed ....... 128 ok collision baked ...... 128 ok sprite atlas ........ 14 sheets, 0 filtered warn 1 sprite exceeds palette (salt_rat_2) ok elapsed ............. 0.9s $ _

What is actually custom

No engine, no framework, no asset pipeline. The renderer draws one rectangle at a time at integer coordinates, which is why nothing has a soft edge anywhere. The map format is a 16-column text file, so the whole of floor 7 fits in a comment in this page.

The soundtrack is one square wave, one triangle, and a noise channel, generated at boot. It is 41 kilobytes and loops for exactly six minutes and twelve seconds.
