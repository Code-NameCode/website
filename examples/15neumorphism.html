Method

Three rules we do not break

  1. Nothing starts suddenly

    Every recording fades in over at least forty seconds. If it made you jump once, it is out of the library.

  2. Nothing changes without warning

    Loop points are crossfaded and marked in the player. No sudden birds, no tempo shift at the join, no volume spike.

  3. Nothing tracks you for using it

    Sleep time is calculated on the device. We never receive when you fell asleep, how long it took, or whether you liked a track.
