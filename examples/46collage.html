Friday is loud

The bill, in no particular order of importance.

Two hundred and forty acts across four stages. Here are the twelve we'd book a flight for, which is a different list from the one on the poster.

Fri

Halloran Fisk

Main stage · Fri 23:40 · 90 min

Dub techno built from field recordings of a decommissioned pumping station. Louder than it has any right to be.

Sat

Ninth Almanac

Tents · Sat 18:15 · 70 min

Six players, one harmonium, and a set list written each morning. Never the same show twice.

Fri

Concrete Orchard

Main stage · Fri 19:20 · 60 min

Post-punk played at the volume of a building site. Very short songs, very long silences.

Sun

Sable Winslow

Long field · Sun 16:00 · 80 min

Ambient, wordless, recorded live in a swimming pool. Bring a mat or a very patient friend.

Sat

Ferrule

Tents · Sat 22:05 · 60 min

Hardware live set with a broken drum machine. The break is the point; do not boo the break.

Sun

The Ridgeway Lights

Long field · Sun 21:00 · 75 min

Folk, drone and a nine-piece choir from Sheffield. Closes the festival; expect confetti.
