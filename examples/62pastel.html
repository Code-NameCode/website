Questions parents actually ask

Before you spend anything

Will these shrink in the wash?

No. We pre-shrink every fabric to 3% before cutting, which is the full amount it moves in a 40° cycle. Wash them a dozen times, measure after the third and you won’t need to measure again.

What if my baby has sensitive skin?

Start with the Nap Muslin and the First Lovey — single-fibre, undyed trims, nothing against the skin that isn’t cotton. If a reaction still happens, we refund everything within 60 days, keepers included.

Is the packaging really plastic-free?

Yes. Mailers are recycled kraft board, tape is paper, and the returns pouch is the same board again. The only plastic anywhere in the box is a thin compostable film on the muslin, which we’re removing next season.

How long does delivery take?

Two to four working days inside the EU, and a flat €4.90 regardless of order size. Outside the EU we use a carbon-offset road freight partner and it takes 6–10 days.

Can I return something after two years?

We only ask you to send it back for repairs if something actually fails. If it simply stopped being used, that’s a fine reason to keep it, and we’d rather you did.
