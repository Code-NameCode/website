Signal path · Model 8

From moving magnet to cone, in seven steps we can name

We publish the whole path because people ask, and because a brand that will not tell you what is between the cartridge and the cone has usually done something interesting there.

  1. CartridgeMoving-magnet and moving-coil, both adjusted from the front panel with a supplied hex key. No proprietary cable, no adapter.

  2. Phono stagePassive-lifted RIAA with a gain switch at 36 or 42 dB, measured rather than calculated.

  3. VolumeA 24-step attenuator with a motorised option for the Model 12, so you can adjust it from the sofa without a remote that costs more than the preamp.

  4. TonePassive Baxandall, switched out of circuit entirely at the centre detent. Nothing is added when you want nothing.

  5. OutputEL34 pairs into a transformer wound by Glyn Mawer in Rotherham, 6 Ω and 4 Ω secondaries, 19 Hz to 42 kHz.

Service

We fix what we made, and a lot we did not

01 · Restoration

Valve amplifiers from 1958 to 1985

Full strip, re-bias, new electrolytics, transformer insulation test, new valves if the originals are beyond specification. Bench report included, photographs included, no charge for the assessment.

02 · Repair

Any brand, any age, in or out of warranty

We have no reason to talk you into a new one. If the repair costs more than half the replacement value, we will tell you that and stop there.

03 · Parts

Bonds stocked for fifteen years

Knobs, valves, feet, transformers, walnut cheeks and the correct 0.22 µF capacitor that nobody else has kept. Sold individually to anyone, not only to owners.
