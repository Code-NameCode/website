What happens

Six rooms, one building, no Wi-Fi

The whole venue is cabled at 56k on purpose. Phones stay in your bag, in a locker, next to the door — and there is a small sign explaining why.

We are not doing a nostalgia party where everything is ironic. The formats are played straight: the tunes, the timings, the broadcast furniture, all of it. The joke is in the room, not in the presentation.

  • 56kEvery room is on a real 56k line, and you will feel it.
  • CRTTwelve 14-inch Trinitrons, kept in service since new.
  • CDTurntables and disc changers, all belt-driven, all serviced.
  • 18+Over-18s. ID at the door, lockers for bags and phones.

Reserve

Ask for a pass, we will hold it for 48 hours

We do not put passes on sale at midnight and then hold them back. What is left is what is left, and we will tell you plainly when it is gone.

Phone ID is checked at the door. The name on the reservation should be the name on the ID, because that is the person who gets in.

Access: step-free from the main entrance to the download room and lobby. The Rack Room is up sixteen steps and up one narrow turn; tell us when you book and we will put you somewhere better.
