What it does

Three jobs, done properly, in the queue where your team already works.

Vaultline is not a chatbot bolted onto a helpdesk. It reads the same history your agents read, so the draft it proposes is one they are willing to send.

One queue, four channels

Email, live chat, phone transcripts and WhatsApp land in the same thread with the same context — including the order history, the previous contacts and the account tier. No copying between tabs, no “let me check and come back”.

  • Unified customer timeline per account
  • Assignment, tags and SLA rules that apply across every channel
  • Full-text search across four years of conversations

Drafts you can actually send

Vaultline proposes a reply from your own past answers, your help centre and the account record. Your agent edits or sends — nothing goes out unread. Every suggestion keeps the sources it used, so a customer can be told where an answer came from.

  • Confidence score on every draft, with the sources listed
  • Hard rules: refunds above a threshold always route to a person
  • Full audit trail of what was drafted, edited and sent

Email support
Does the assistant ever send a reply on its own?
Only where you switch it on, and only for conversations you have marked as routine. Refunds, complaints, anything mentioning legal or a regulator, and any message from a VIP tier always stop at a human. You can set the threshold per queue.
What happens to our existing helpdesk history?
We import conversations, tags and macros from Zendesk, Front and Freshdesk in a first-night migration, keeping their timestamps. Nothing is deleted from the source system; we only read until you are satisfied.
Where is customer data stored?
EU, UK or US regions on the plan you choose, encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.3. Scale and Enterprise plans can pin conversations to a single residency region and export on request.
Can we keep our own email domain?
Yes, and most teams do. You authenticate the domain once, then Vaultline sends and receives as support@yourdomain.com with the SPF, DKIM and DMARC records we generate for you.
What does rollout actually involve?
Two weeks is the median, not a rule. Week one is connecting channels and importing history. Week two is turning the assistant on for one queue at low confidence, then widening once the team trusts what it drafts.
