Sport sessions for ages 5–12

Move. Jump.

Bounce Lab runs Saturday and after-school sessions where children try eight sports in one hour, on purpose, with the rules bent just enough that everyone gets a turn. Coaches are qualified, ratios are strict, and nobody sits on the bench.

What an hour actually looks like

1

Arrive and pick a name tag

Name tags are mandatory, in a colour that changes weekly. It stops the same three children being picked first, every time, forever.

5 min
2

Warm up as a group

One song, eight movements, no exceptions. Coaches model every one of them badly first so nobody feels like they're bad at it.

8 min
3

Eight stations, two each

Four minutes per station, buzzer moves everyone. A coach stays at the station; nobody queues to watch.

32 min
4

Cool down and say what was good

One thing that went well, said out loud, about someone else. Then the wall chart, then parents, then home.

10 min

For parents and carers

The questions we get asked in the car park, answered properly rather than on a leaflet nobody reads.

Does my child need to be sporty?+

No. About a third of the children who start have never done organised sport. The single rule is that you try, and we do not keep score for anyone under eight.

What should they wear and bring?+

Trainers with laces tied by an adult, clothes they don't mind ruining, and a water bottle we will not fill for you. We keep a small box of spare hair ties and a jumper that has been here since 2019.

Is it safe?+

Every coach holds a current enhanced DBS check and paediatric first aid. Ratios are 1:6 for Cubs, 1:8 for Squad and 1:10 for Lab, and those are the actual numbers, not the ones on the poster.

What if they are neurodivergent or anxious?+

Tell us and we will plan it. Quiet arrival at 09:05 instead of 09:00, a visual timetable emailed the night before, a coach who knows to say less. Several families do this with us and none of it is flagged to the other parents.

Can we pause or pay monthly?+

Yes to both, and no questions. Life moves; we would rather have you back in January than lose you permanently. Monthly is £12 a month per child and cancels with 30 days' notice.
