How it happens

Three steps, one short morning.

Everything we make is time-limited, so the process is deliberately small. No warehouses, no holding tanks, no overnight hangovers.

  1. Picked at dawn

    Our growers pick only what ripens that day, into shallow trays so nothing is crushed on the way in.

  2. Pressed within the hour

    A slow hydraulic press, 4 tonnes per square inch. The heat from friction never gets a chance to cook the colour out.

  3. Bottled in glass, sent same day

    Unpasteurised means a 12-day life. We label it honestly, and the courier van leaves at four.
