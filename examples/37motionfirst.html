Motion system

How this page moves, and why

Animation here is not decoration. It does three jobs: it tells you something arrived, it tells you what changed as a result of you, and it never sits between you and the task.

Every duration below is a token. Nothing on this page uses a number that is not in that list.

Durations and curves
TokenDurationUsed for
--enter-fast180 msPress, check, hover, state acknowledgement180
--enter-base420 msAny component arriving on scroll420
--enter-slow720 msPanel enter and leave720
--stage1 200 msOne full tour step, progress bar1 200
--stagger80 msPer item inside a stagger group80
Entering
cubic-bezier(.16, 1, .3, 1) — decelerates, arrives softly
Leaving
cubic-bezier(.5, 0, .75, 0) — accelerates out, never lingers
Reduced motion
All of it collapses to 0.01 ms. Panels swap in place, callouts do not appear.
