02 — Programme

Four days, twenty-six screenings

Every screening is a single 35 mm or 4K print. No second cuts, no streaming substitutes, no encore of things you have already seen.

  • Kino Aurora · Hall 1 · English subtitlesA fisherwoman's ledgers are read aloud by the daughter she left them to. Adeyemi's second feature, shot on 16 mm over two monsoon seasons.

  • Basin Hall · Live score by the Vantar QuartetForty years of a meteorological station, told entirely through the people who kept the log books. Restored premiere.

  • Kino Aurora · Hall 2 · Norwegian subtitlesA crew of glacier surveyors spend a final season on a sheet they know is going. No interviews, no narration, eleven hours of weather.

  • Kino Aurora · Hall 1 · Korean subtitlesThree generations of one family run a bakery that only opens before dawn. Lim's camera rarely leaves the counter.

  • Basin Hall · 24 fps print, no intermissionA ferry captain replays the same crossing for two hours and twenty minutes, and slowly the route becomes a memory of a different sea.

  • Kino Aurora · Hall 2 · Swahili subtitlesFive houses scheduled for demolition let their residents narrate the walls. Shot in a single day.

  • Basin Hall · North American premiereA school holds a dance nobody attends for three and a half hours. The year's most patient film.

  • Kino Aurora · Hall 1 · Subtitles on all filmsOur opening shorts block, all nine selected from 2 140 submissions. The winner closes the festival's competition strand.
