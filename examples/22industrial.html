02 / Cylinders & manifolds

Bodies built from
forged bar stock

Cylinder barrels are forged, honed and pressure-tested to 1.5× working pressure before the first component is assembled. Standard bore sizes are stocked; anything else is built to your drawing with a three-week lead time.

Ordering path

  1. Send a bore, stroke and rod-Ø, or a drawing — a photo on a phone is enough to start.
  2. Our applications engineer returns a cut sheet with side-load limits and a projected life figure.
  3. You confirm the mounting style; we machine the end caps to suit.
  4. Bench test, then crate. Tracking and test certificates travel with the pallet.
What does “1.5× working pressure” mean in practice?
Every barrel is filled with a fluid and held at one-and-a-half times its rated working pressure for ten minutes. The test is destructive on a sample from each heat lot, and on a non-destructive check on your unit. The certificate records the lot number, the medium and the ambient temperature.
Do you repair cylinders you did not build?
Yes — we re-bore, hone and re-chrome rods up to 200 mm bore. Send dimensions and a photo of the mounting flange; we will confirm in writing before you ship anything.
Which fluids are supported?
Standard mineral oil, HFC and fire-resistant phosphate ester. Bio-based fluids need a seal-material change, which we quote as a separate line item rather than burying it in the price.
Lead times when a part is out of stock
Stocked bores ship in five working days. Non-standard bores run three weeks; forged barrel stock is bought in against a rolling four-week forecast, which is why we ask for a forecast rather than a one-off.
