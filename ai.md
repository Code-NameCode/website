## The rules we build by

- **Human control:** approvals, sensitive account actions, security decisions, billing commitments and final customer communication stay with your team until a safer operating model is proven. AI prepares; people decide.
- **Explainability:** every workflow has a written map: what triggers it, what data it reads, what it produces, and where a person checks the output. If it cannot be explained on one page, it does not ship.
- **Permissioning:** agents get the least access that does the job - scoped accounts, scoped data, scoped actions. Credentials live in your name.
- **Honest boundaries:** we say plainly when a task should not be automated, when accuracy is not good enough yet, and when a human does it better.
- **Ownership:** documents, prompts, accounts and configurations are handed over in writing.

## How an AI engagement runs

1. **Map** - the workflow in plain language: what happens today, where the hours go, where mistakes happen.
2. **Set boundaries** - what the system may do, what it must never do, and where a person checks the output. In writing.
3. **Launch with oversight** - the workflow runs on real work with a human checkpoint, measured against the task.
4. **Hand over or expand** - once it earns trust we document and hand it over, then move to the next task.
