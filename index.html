Before you ask.

The questions that come up on nearly every first call - answered plainly.

Do you offer managed IT support outside Heidelberg?

Yes - remote IT support anywhere in South Africa and for international clients, with on-site support across the Vaal Triangle and East Rand. Critical issues target a response within four hours during business hours.

Can you run our Google Ads and social media marketing?

Yes. We manage Google Ads campaigns and social media on Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube and X, and set up Google Analytics 4, Search Console and Tag Manager so every channel is judged on cost per lead, not likes. More on digital marketing.

How much does a business website cost in South Africa?

Business websites start from R5,000 once-off and e-commerce stores from R8,500, with fixed quotes before work starts. Managed Linux hosting runs from R100 per month. Websites & hosting detail.

What is included with your managed hosting?

Every plan runs on managed Linux with the DirectAdmin control panel, SSL certificates included, email hosting and a 99% uptime target - and we migrate your existing site as part of the conversation, not as an extra project.

Can AI automation actually help a small business?

When the task repeats, yes. We map one workflow - inbox triage, report drafting, lead capture - automate it with human checkpoints, and hand over documentation you own. If AI is the wrong tool for the task, we say so. How we approach AI.
