Before you ask.

The questions that come up on nearly every website and hosting call.

We already have hosting. Can you move the site?

Yes-migration is part of the conversation, not an extra project. We move the site, check it on the new plan, and only then touch the DNS, so the old site keeps running until the moment the new one is live.

Do I own the domain and hosting account?

You do. Everything is registered in your name and the credentials are handed over in writing. You stay because the hosting is good, not because you can't leave.

What happens if the site outgrows the plan?

We tell you before it becomes a problem, and an upgrade is a setting change-not a migration. You only pay for the tier the site actually needs.

Is email hosting included?

Yes, on every plan, with Business and Pro sized for proper business mailbox use. We also handle the DNS records that make email deliver.

Can you take over a site someone else built?

Usually, yes. We assess what the site runs on, quote the takeover honestly, and tell you when a rebuild is cheaper than a rescue.

How styles combine

A combination only earns its place when it serves the content or the brand. Name one dominant style and no more than two supporting ones. The primary style sets structure, grid, spacing and type rhythm; a secondary style shapes content presentation and tone; an accent style is confined to small details. Tokens must not fight: at most two font families, one base palette, one radius logic, one depth model, one motion language.
