# Goodstuff Designs - Websites & Managed Hosting

Goodstuff Designs designs, builds, hosts and maintains websites. Based in Heidelberg, South Africa, working with clients across South Africa and internationally.

## Three kinds of build

- **Business website** - presence and credibility, structured for search from day one. Once-off from R5,000.
- **E-commerce** - products, cart and checkout built for clarity on a phone. Once-off from R8,500.
- **Custom web apps** - dashboards, booking systems, internal tools and point-of-sale interfaces. Scoped and quoted.

Final scope, price, content, integrations, and timing must be confirmed in writing.

## Included in every build

- Mobile and tablet first
- Search-ready structure: sitemaps, metadata, clean URLs
- Google Analytics 4 and Google Search Console configured, connected and verified
- Domain, hosting and accounts registered in the client's name, credentials handed over in writing
- Hosting that fits, from R100/month, with migration handled
- Support after launch from the same team that built it

## Delivery track

1. **Scope** - what the site must do and for whom, quoted in writing.
2. **Design** - layout, structure and words reviewed before production code.
3. **Build** - tested on real phones and tablets, previewed as it takes shape.
4. **Launch & after** - DNS, hosting, analytics and search submission handled; ongoing updates and backups on request.

## FAQ

- **We already have hosting. Can you move the site?** Yes - migration is part of the conversation, not an extra project. The site moves, is checked on the new plan, and only then does DNS change.
- **Do I own the domain and hosting account?** You do. Registered in your name, credentials handed over in writing.
- **What happens if the site outgrows the plan?** You are told before it becomes a problem; an upgrade is a setting change, not a migration.
- **Is email hosting included?** Yes, on every plan, with Business and Pro sized for proper business mailbox use.
- **Can you take over a site someone else built?** Usually, yes. The takeover is assessed and quoted honestly - including when a rebuild is cheaper than a rescue.
