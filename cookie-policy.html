What cookies do

Cookies are small values stored by a browser. This site uses one essential cookie to remember your decision. If you accept analytics, Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity may use additional cookies to connect measurements across visits.

Your choice

Accept analytics enables analytics storage. Reject leaves analytics storage denied. Advertising storage, advertising user data and advertising personalisation remain denied in both cases.

Because this site uses Advanced Consent Mode, Google and Microsoft may receive limited cookieless measurements when analytics is rejected. These signals are designed to operate without analytics cookies and do not create a consented cross-page Clarity session.

Measurement

We measure page views and meaningful enquiry actions: support, project and hosting calls to action; email and telephone links; hosting-plan selections; and outbound social links. Event metadata uses general categories only. It must not contain enquiry text, names, contact details or message subjects.

Changing your mind

Use Cookie settings in any page footer. Rejecting after previously accepting updates consent immediately, asks Clarity to clear its session, and removes accessible first-party Google Analytics and Clarity cookies. Browser controls can also block or delete cookies, although doing so may cause this banner to appear again.
