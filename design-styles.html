How to use this reference

A style is a starting decision, not a finish line. In practice one dominant style sets the structure, grid, spacing and type rhythm; a second may shape content presentation; a third is confined to small details. Mixing works when the tokens do not fight - at most two font families, one base palette, one radius logic, one depth model. Tell us which direction fits the business and we will build from there.
